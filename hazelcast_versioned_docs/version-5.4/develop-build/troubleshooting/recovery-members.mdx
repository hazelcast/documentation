---
title: "Recovery from a Partial or Total Failure"
sidebar_position: 4
slug: /troubleshoot/recovery-members
---

Under normal circumstances, Hazelcast members are self-recoverable as in the following scenarios:

- Automatic split-brain resolution
- Hazelcast allowing stuck/unreachable members to come back within configured tolerance levels; see the [Configuring for Fault Tolerance section](../../operate-manage/configuring-for-fault-tolerance/backups.mdx).

However, in the rare case when a member is declared unreachable by Hazelcast because it fails to respond, but the rest of the cluster is still running, consider the followings for recovery:

- Collect Hazelcast [logs](../../operate-manage/logging.mdx) from all members, active and unresponsive.
- Collect Hazelcast client [logs](../../operate-manage/logging.mdx) or application logs from all clients.
- If the cluster is running and one or more members were ejected from the cluster because it was stuck, take a [heap dump](./remedies-for-alerts.mdx#heap-dumps) and [thread-dump](./remedies-for-alerts.mdx#thread-dumps) of any stuck members.
- After collecting all the necessary artifacts, shut down the ailing members by calling shutdown hooks; see the [Shutting Down the Cluster section](../../operate-manage/maintaining-a-cluster/shutdown.mdx).
- After shutdown, start the ailing members and wait for them to join the cluster. After successfully joining, Hazelcast rebalances the data across the members.
