The previous method of dynamically loading custom classes or domain classes from other members, or deploying your code from your client was known as <Variable name="ucd" />. This method has been deprecated and will be removed at the next major version. The legacy information for the deprecated <Variable name="ucd" /> solution has been retained in this section for your reference.

<Variable name="ucn" /> is only available to <Variable name="enterprise-product-name" /> users.

Hazelcast recommends that <Variable name="enterprise-product-name" /> users migrate to <Variable name="ucn" /> to benefit from the improved and extended capabilities that it offers.

After the next major release, <Variable name="open-source-product-name" /> users can either upgrade to <Variable name="enterprise-product-name" />, or add their resources to the Hazelcast member class paths.

For further information on migrating from <Variable name="ucd" /> to <Variable name="ucn" />, see the [Migrate from User Code Deployment](../ucn-migrate-ucd.mdx) topic.

For further information on upgrading to Hazelcast Platform from IMDG, see the [Upgrading from IMDG 3.12.x](../../../install-and-upgrade/upgrade/upgrading-from-imdg-3.mdx) or [Upgrading from IMDG 4.x](../../../install-and-upgrade/upgrade/upgrading-from-imdg-4.mdx) topics.
