From a <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Standard cluster, you can stream the last 1,000 lines of your cluster logs, using the Maven plugin.

1. [Configure the Maven plugin](../../reference/maven-plugin-hazelcast.mdx) to connect to your cluster.
2. Execute the following:

   ```bash
   mvn hazelcast-cloud:stream-logs
   ```

You'll see the latest 1,000 lines of logs for a single member in your cluster.

## Next Steps

To see the logs of all members in your cluster, see [Downloading Cluster Logs](./download-logs.mdx).
