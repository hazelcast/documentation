Cluster logs are useful for monitoring your cluster or troubleshooting issues. Depending on your cluster edition, you can stream cluster logs directly to your IDE, download them, or integrate with a log management system.

## Logs for <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Standard Clusters {/* #logs-for-cloud-standard-clusters */}

To view logs for <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Standard clusters, you can use the following options:

- [Stream logs from a single cluster](/cloud/stream-logs).
- [Download logs from all clusters](/cloud/download-logs).

## Logs for <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Dedicated Clusters {/* #logs-for-cloud-dedicated-clusters */}

You can [download logs](/cloud/download-logs) from all <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Dedicated clusters.
