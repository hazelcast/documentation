To communicate with a cluster, you need a client. Hazelcast offers clients in a variety of languages as well as a command-line client for quick prototyping and scripting.

You can do the following to connect to the cluster:

- Download a [preconfigured sample client](#download).
- Use [advanced setup](#advanced) to configure your own client.
- Use the [SQL browser](#sql) in Management Center.

## Before you Begin

If you’re using a cluster on Cloud Dedicated, you need to [authorize](/cloud/authorize-connections) clients to allow them to connect to your cluster.

## Using a Preconfigured Client {/* #download */}

Your cluster comes with the following clients, which are preconfigured to connect to your cluster:

- [Java](/hazelcast/5.7/clients/java#configuring-java-client)
- [Python](https://hazelcast.readthedocs.io/en/stable/configuration_overview.html)
- [.NET](http://hazelcast.github.io/hazelcast-csharp-client/latest/doc/index.html)
- [Hazelcast Command-Line Client (CLC)](/clc/5.5.1/overview)

To download any of these clients, complete the following steps:

1. Sign in to the [Cloud console](https://cloud.hazelcast.com/)
2. Select your cluster.
3. Select the relevant option in the **Connect Client** section of your Dashboard.

   :::note

   To download the CLC client, select the **CLI** option.

   :::
4. Follow the on-screen instructions.

For a complete tutorial, see the [Cloud Hello World](/cloud/get-started) topic.

## Configure Your Own Client {/* #advanced */}

You can access the information needed to connect your own clients through the **Advanced** option.

To obtain the connection credentials for your cluster, complete the following steps:

1. Sign in to the [Cloud console](https://cloud.hazelcast.com/).
2. Select your cluster.
3. Select the **Advanced** option in the **Connect Client** section of your Dashboard.

   The connection credentials display.

## Using the SQL Browser {/* #sql */}

You can open a SQL shell in your cluster and submit SQL queries directly from your web browser.

1. Sign into the [Cloud console](https://cloud.hazelcast.com/) and select your cluster.
2. Go to **SQL**.
3. Enter your SQL queries.

For full details see [Executing SQL Queries](/cloud/execute-sql-queries).
