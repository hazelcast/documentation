## View Billing and Payment Summary {/* #view-billing-and-payment-summary */}

See how much you’ve spent in the current month and how much you spent in previous months.

To view your spending information, complete the following steps:

1. Sign in to the [Cloud console](https://cloud.hazelcast.com/).
2. Select **Account** from the side navigation bar
3. Select **Organization** from the **Account** options

   The Info screen displays.
4. Select the **Billing & Payments** tab

   The Billing & Payment screen displays.

On this screen, You can view how much you’ve spent, your invoice history, and payment method at any time.

You can also [update your payment method](/cloud/update-payment-method) and [create or edit a budget tracker](/cloud/create-budget-tracker).
