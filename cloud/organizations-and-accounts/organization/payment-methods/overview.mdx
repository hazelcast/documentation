When you sign up for the Standard or Dedicated Edition of <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/>, you must provide a payment method. You can view a summary of your invoices and usage at any time.

When you sign up for a Standard or Dedicated <Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> account, you must add a payment method as follows:

- For <a href="/cloud/serverless-cluster"><Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Standard clusters</a>, you are prompted to add your payment details after choosing to create a Development or Production cluster during sign-up. You must provide a payment card to create clusters. For further information on adding a payment method, see the [Add Payment Method](../create-account.mdx#add-payment) section of the Create an Account topic
- For <a href="/cloud/dedicated-cluster"><Variable name="hazelcast-cloud"/> Dedicated clusters</a>, contact us to discuss your requirement and costs; the payment method is set up as part of this process. Alternatively, you can buy a three-member (13.5GB of storage) package directly from the [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-olxywxtjninay) Payment options include the following:

  - Card payments
  - Purchase orders

Once your account and payment method is set up, you can do the following:

- View your usage and associated costs, along with your payment history, as described in the [View Usage and Bills](./view-usage-and-bills.mdx) topic
- Update your payment method, as described in the [Update Payment Method](./update-payment-method.mdx) topic
- Create a budget tracker, as described in the [Create or Edit Budget Tracker](./create-budget-tracker.mdx) topic
