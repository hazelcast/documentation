You can use Management Center to monitor your cluster. Management Center allows you to view insights about cluster state, data structure metrics, and other metrics.

## Opening Management Center

To open Management Center, do the following:

1. Sign into the <Variable href="{page-cloud-console}" text="{hazelcast-cloud} console"/> and select your cluster.
2. In **Cluster details**, click **Management Center**.

## Related Resources

To learn how to use Management Center, see the [Management Center documentation](/management-center/5.11/getting-started/overview).
