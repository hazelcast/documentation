If you no longer need a cluster, you can delete it.

## Using the Cloud Console

1. Sign into the <LinkVariable name="page-cloud-console">Cloud console</LinkVariable> and select your cluster.
2. Click **Delete** and confirm your choice.

When you delete a cluster, all data related to the cluster, including the configuration, is removed. You cannot recover any data from a deleted cluster.
