If your clusters are exposed to the Internet, they allow any authenticated client to connect to them by default. To grant access only to specific clients, you can add their IP addresses to a whitelist.

1. Sign into the <Variable href="{page-cloud-console}" text="{hazelcast-cloud} console" /> and select your cluster.
2. Go to **Manage** \> **IP Whitelisting**.
3. Make sure that the **Enable** toggle is **On** in the top-right corner.
4. Add the IP addresses of your clients.
5. Click **Save Configuration**.

Any changes you make will be effective immediately.
