To recover a cluster that has [Persistence](./persistence.mdx) enabled after a cluster-wide restart, you can force a cluster to delete their persistence stores when one or more members fail to restart.

## Before you Begin

You should trigger a force-start only if your cluster has Persistence enabled and it is configured to use the `FULL_RECOVERY_ONLY` policy. See [Choosing a Cluster Recovery Policy](./configuring-persistence.mdx#policy).

:::warning

Force-start deletes all data in your cluster members' persistence stores.

:::
