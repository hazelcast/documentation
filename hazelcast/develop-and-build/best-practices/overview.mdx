This section provides guidance on optimizing your Hazelcast deployment for performance, reliability, and operational efficiency. The following topics cover essential best practices across multiple areas:

- [Capacity planning](./capacity-planning.mdx) — for resource estimation and cluster sizing.
- [Performance tips](./performance-tips.mdx) — for tuning your production environment.
- [Back pressure](./back-pressure.mdx) and [Pipelining](./pipelining.mdx) — for optimizing throughput.
- [AWS deployments](./aws-deployments.mdx) — for cloud-specific considerations.
- [Threading](./threading.mdx) — for efficient operation execution.
- [Near Cache](./near-cache.mdx) — for reducing latency.
- [`IMap` bulk read operations](./imap-bulk-read-operations.mdx) — for preventing out-of-memory issues.
- [Data affinity](./data-affinity.mdx) — for optimizing data locality.
- [Security best practices](./security-best-practices.mdx) — for planning and optimizing security.

Additionally, refer to [Testing best practices](../test-applications/testing-bestpractices.mdx) for guidance on writing reliable and efficient tests for your Hazelcast applications, and [CP Subsystem best practices](../../operate-and-manage/cp-subsystem/best-practices.mdx) to get the best performance out of your CP Subsystem deployment.
