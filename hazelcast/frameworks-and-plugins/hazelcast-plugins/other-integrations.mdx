Apart from the officially maintained integrations listed above, there are a number of Hazelcast community plugins:

- [Hazelcast Connector for Kafka](https://lenses.io/connect/kafka-to-hazelcast/)
- [Hazelcast Connector for Apache Spark](https://github.com/hazelcast/hazelcast-spark)
- [Hazelcast Mesos](https://github.com/hazelcast/hazelcast-mesos)
- [Hazelcast Openfire integration](https://www.igniterealtime.org/projects/openfire/plugins/hazelcast/readme.html)
- [Hazelcast Grails plugin](https://github.com/hazelcast/hazelcast-grails)
- [Hazelcast SubZero serialization](https://github.com/jerrinot/subzero)
