The console feature enables you to execute commands on the cluster. For example, you can perform `put` and `get` operations on a map.

The console is not recommended for production use.

  1. Open a console window by clicking on the Console button located on the left panel or the one located at the top menu.

    You can also open the console, using the kbd::[Alt + T] keyboard shortcut on Windows/Linux and kbd::[Option + T] on macOS.

  2. Set the namespace of a data structure, using the ns <name of your data structure> command.

    The console does not support data structures with spaces or special characters in their names.

To get help or a list of all possible commands, enter help in the console.
