Maintaining a cluster often requires shutting down and restarting members to make changes take effect. You can shut down a whole Hazelcast cluster or individual members in Management Center with one button.

## Shut down a cluster

To gracefully shut down a cluster, go to **Administration** \> **Cluster State** and click **Shutdown**.

## Shut down a member

To gracefully shut down a member:

1. Go to **Members**.
2. Click a member's row in the table.
3. Click **Shutdown** in the top right-hand corner.
