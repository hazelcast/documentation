You can see a list of all the <Variable href="/hazelcast/{page-latest-supported-hazelcast}/data-structures/cpmap" text="CPMaps"/> in your cluster by clicking on the **CPMaps** menu item in the left menu.

![CPMaps View](../images/CPMaps.png)

A CPMap has two metrics:

- **Entries**: The number of entries in the map
- **Size**: The size of the map in memory

Sort the table by clicking on the column headers.
